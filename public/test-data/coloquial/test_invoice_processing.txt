This process describes test_invoice_processing.

The process begins when Invoice Received by AccountsPayable.
After this (invoice_received), the process moves to Validate Invoice.
Next, Validate Invoice (System) executes a service.
After this (validated), the process moves to Parallel Split.
The process splits into parallel paths at Parallel Split (AccountsPayable).
  - One path goes to Verify Amount (fork_amount).
  Next, Verify Amount (Finance) executes a service.
  After this (amount_verified), the process moves to Parallel Join.
  The parallel paths join at Parallel Join (AccountsPayable).
  After this (sync), the process moves to Process Payment.
    Next, Process Payment (Finance) executes a service.
    After this (payment_complete), the process moves to Invoice Paid.
  - One path goes to Check Vendor (fork_vendor).
  Next, Check Vendor (VendorManagement) executes a service.
  After this (vendor_verified), the process moves to Parallel Join.
  The parallel paths join at Parallel Join (AccountsPayable).
  After this (sync), the process moves to Process Payment.
    Next, Process Payment (Finance) executes a service.
    After this (payment_complete), the process moves to Invoice Paid.
  - One path goes to Approve Payment (fork_approval).
  Next, Approve Payment (Manager) requires user action.
  After this (approved), the process moves to Parallel Join.
  The parallel paths join at Parallel Join (AccountsPayable).
  After this (sync), the process moves to Process Payment.
    Next, Process Payment (Finance) executes a service.
    After this (payment_complete), the process moves to Invoice Paid.

The process completes when Invoice Paid is reached.
