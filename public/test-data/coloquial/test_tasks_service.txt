This process describes test_tasks_service.

The process begins when Start by System.
After this (trigger_validation), the process moves to Validate Data.
Next, Validate Data (System) executes a service.
After this (validation_complete), the process moves to Send Notification.
Next, Send Notification (System) executes a service.
After this (notification_sent), the process moves to End.

The process completes when End is reached.
