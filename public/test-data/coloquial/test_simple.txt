This process describes Simple Process.

The process begins when Start.
After this (Start Process), the process moves to Task1.
Next, Task1 (User) performs a task.
After this (Complete Task), the process moves to Decision.
At Decision, a decision is made:
  - The process continues to Task2 (Path A) if condition == true.
  Next, Task2 (User) performs a task.
  After this (Complete), the process moves to End.
  - The process continues to Task3 (Path B) if condition == false.
  Next, Task3 (User) performs a task.
  After this (Complete), the process moves to End.

The process completes when End is reached.

The process uses the following variables:
  - condition (bool)
