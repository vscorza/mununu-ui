This process describes test_compensation.

The process begins when Start by Process.
After this (begin), the process moves to Book Flight.
Next, Book Flight (Booking) executes a service.
After this (flight_booked), the process moves to Book Hotel.
Next, Book Hotel (Booking) executes a service.
Next, Error Occurred (Process) triggers an intermediate event.
After this (compensate_hotel), the process moves to Cancel Hotel.
Next, Cancel Hotel (Booking) executes a service.
After this (hotel_cancelled), the process moves to Cancel Flight.
Next, Cancel Flight (Booking) executes a service.
After this (flight_cancelled), the process moves to End.

The process completes when End is reached.
