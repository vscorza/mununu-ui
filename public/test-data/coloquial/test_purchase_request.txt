This process describes test_purchase_request.

The process begins when Submit Request by Employee.
After this (submit), the process moves to Check Budget.
At Check Budget (Finance), a decision is made:
  - The process continues to Manager Approval (within_budget) if amount <= 5000.
  Next, Manager Approval (Manager) requires user action.
  After this (reviewed), the process moves to Manager Decision.
  At Manager Decision (Manager), a decision is made:
    - The process continues to Process Purchase (approved) if this condition is met.
    Next, Process Purchase (Procurement) executes a service.
    After this (purchase_complete), the process moves to Approved.
    - The process continues to Rejected (rejected) if this condition is met.
  - The process continues to C F O Approval (exceeds_budget) if amount > 5000.
  Next, C F O Approval (CFO) requires user action.
  After this (reviewed), the process moves to C F O Decision.
  At C F O Decision (CFO), a decision is made:
    - The process continues to Process Purchase (approved) if this condition is met.
    Next, Process Purchase (Procurement) executes a service.
    After this (purchase_complete), the process moves to Approved.
    - The process continues to Rejected (rejected) if this condition is met.

The process can complete in 2 different ways:
  - Rejected
  - Approved
