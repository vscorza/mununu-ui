This process describes test_cycles.

The process begins when Start by Process.
After this (begin), the process moves to Process Item.
Next, Process Item (Processor) requires user action.
After this (item_processed), the process moves to Check Complete.
At Check Complete (Processor), a decision is made:
  - The process continues to More Items (has_more) if items_remaining > 0.
  Next, More Items (Processor) performs a task.
  After this (next_item), the process moves to Process Item.
  - The process continues to End (all_done) if items_remaining == 0.

The process completes when End is reached.
